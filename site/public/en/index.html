About

Romeo — Balloon Artist

With 14 years of experience, Romeo has turned balloon art into a live show. His repertoire spans more than 100 sculptures — characters, animals, hats, giant pieces and themed creations, made on the spot in front of children and guests.

Based in São Paulo, Romeo performs at children's parties, store events, restaurants and large family events — such as SESI Diverte (Vila Leopoldina), where the event coordinators wrote: “Your work delighted our children and families, and the attraction was a huge success!”

A flower, a puppy and a sword? That's just the beginning.

Romeo is from China and works in Portuguese, English and Chinese — just send a WhatsApp message in any of them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the duration, number of guests and party location. Message me on WhatsApp with the date and area and I'll send a quote right away.

Which areas do you cover?

São Paulo and the metropolitan region. Send me the party address and I'll confirm travel.

What's included?

All balloons and materials, the live performance for the booked time and, if you like, the clown costume.

Can the kids choose what they want?

Yes! I have over 100 sculptures in my repertoire — characters, animals, party themes. They choose and I make it on the spot.

How do I book?

With a 50% deposit via Pix. The balance is paid on the day of the event. As soon as the deposit arrives, the date is yours! 🎈

Do you also do decorations?

I make custom themed sculptures for decor and a giant themed sculpture as a photo spot.
